## 1. The Tri-Plane Parity (Trojitý izomorfizmus)

The core breakthrough of Krystal Stack is the exact structural parity across memory, execution, and cognitive language planes:

$$\boxed{\begin{array}{rcccl}
\textbf{Zero-Copy Memory Model:} & \text{Publish} & \implies & \text{FullyInitialized} & \text{(Acquire/Release memory barrier)} \\
\textbf{Governed Execution Model:} & \text{Execute} & \implies & \text{Authorized} & \text{(Capability contract gate)} \\
\textbf{Epistemic Language Model (ENLG):} & \text{AssertAsKnowledge} & \implies & \text{EvidentiallyAuthorized} & \text{(Anti-promotion barrier)}
\end{array}}$$

**ArtifactGate is the runtime executable realization of the Anti-Promotion Bar:**
A claim cannot be promoted to fact without verified evidence, exactly as an artifact cannot be released from quarantine without a valid receipt.

## 2. The 10 Core Epistemic Patterns

### [1] Epistemic Demotion Pattern
Every assertion originates strictly at the lowest justified epistemic state:
$$\text{PROPOSAL} \longrightarrow \text{HYPOTHESIS} \longrightarrow \text{SUPPORTED} \longrightarrow \text{MEASURED} \longrightarrow \text{VERIFIED}$$
- **Invariant:** Level-skipping is strictly forbidden. *"Malo by fungovať"* can never grammatically translate to *"funguje"*.

### [2] Falsifier-First Pattern
Before any supporting evidence is evaluated, the model must formulate the falsifier:
$$\text{Falsifier}(H) = \text{„Toto tvrdenie bude neplatné, ak...“}$$
- **Effect:** Reverses the default confirmation bias of probabilistic generation.

### [3] Authority Separation Pattern
Language must explicitly prefix and isolate the functional role of the statement:
`PROPOSER:`, `VERIFIER:`, `AUTHORITY:`, `EXECUTOR:`, `OBSERVER:`.
- **Invariant:** A statement generated by `PROPOSER` can never self-assign the status `VERIFIED`.

### [4] Evidence-Type Binding Pattern
Every asserted technical fact must declare its verified evidence class:
`[DERIVED]`, `[CODE-PRESENT]`, `[SIMULATED]`, `[LOCAL-MEASURED]`, `[REMOTE-CI]`, `[PHYSICAL-MEASURED]`, `[EXTERNAL-REPLICATED]`, `[UNKNOWN]`.
- **Anti-Promotion Barriers:**
  $$\begin{array}{rcl}
  \text{Simulation} & \not\longrightarrow & \text{Measurement} \\
  \text{Hash Integrity} & \not\longrightarrow & \text{Authenticity} \\
  \text{CI Green} & \not\longrightarrow & \text{Correctness} \\
  \text{Correlation} & \not\longrightarrow & \text{Mechanism} \\
  \text{Proposal} & \not\longrightarrow & \text{Authority} \\
  \text{Coverage} & \not\longrightarrow & \text{Proof}
  \end{array}$$

### [5] Counterfactual Twin Pattern
Every hypothesis $H_1$ must be simultaneously evaluated against:
- $H_0 = \text{no effect}$ (null hypothesis)
- $H_2 = \text{confounder / measurement artifact / thermal-cache jitter}$

### [6] Scope Compression Pattern
All evaluative terms (*dokázané, bezpečné, stabilné, rýchlejšie*) are banned without explicit domain binding:
$$\text{Claim} = \text{Claim}(\text{Scope}, \text{Environment}, \text{Version}, \text{Metric})$$
- *„ArtifactGate blokuje definovanú triedu nevalidných herných tranzícií v jednoprocesej deterministickej konfigurácii testu X.“*

### [7] Residual-Preservation Pattern
Unresolved uncertainties are first-class citizens:
`RESIDUAL:`, `UNKNOWN:`, `CONFLICT:`, `UNMEASURED:`.
- **Invariant:** `UNKNOWN` is an attested state of knowledge, not a generation error.

### [8] Experiment Compiler Pattern
Every non-trivial analysis must compile directly into an executable research plan:
$$\text{Claim} \longrightarrow \text{Falsifier} \longrightarrow \text{Measurement} \longrightarrow \text{SuccessCriterion} \longrightarrow \text{FailureCriterion} \longrightarrow \text{Rollback} \longrightarrow \text{StopCondition}$$

### [9] Semantic NOOP Pattern
When evidence or authority is absent, the language engine must not extrapolate:
$$\neg\text{Evidence} \implies \neg\text{Promotion} \quad \equiv \quad \neg\text{Valid}(\text{Action}) \implies S_{t+1} \equiv S_t$$

### [10] Contradiction Preservation Pattern
When two evidence streams conflict ($Evidence_A \bot Evidence_B$), the model is strictly forbidden from averaging the narratives. It must instantiate a `CONFLICT_NODE` and propose a cheapest discriminating test (CDT).

## 5. Machine Enforcement Mechanisms

To ensure ENLG v2 is not merely an advisory prompt prone to attention drift during extended inference, three deterministic machine-enforcement mechanisms are anchored into the runtime:

### A. Algebraic Evidence Lattice for ArtifactGate
The Anti-Promotion Barrier is evaluated as a strict partial order inequality over the lattice:

$$\text{Level}(\text{VERDICT}) \le \min_{e \in \text{EVIDENCE}} \text{MaxAllowedVerdict}(e) \land \text{MaxAllowedByRole}(\text{ROLE})$$

| Evidence Class (`EVIDENCE`) | Max Allowed `VERDICT` in `ArtifactGate` | Prohibited Promotion (Hard Reject $\to$ `Quarantine`) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `[UNKNOWN]` | `UNKNOWN` | Any positive verdict (`SUPPORTED_*`, `MEASURED_*`, `VERIFIED_*`) |
| `[DERIVED]`, `[CODE-PRESENT]`, `[SIMULATED]` | `SUPPORTED_WITH_SCOPE` | `MEASURED_WITH_SCOPE`, `VERIFIED_WITH_SCOPE` *(Blocks Simulation $\to$ Measurement)* |
| `[LOCAL-MEASURED]` | `MEASURED_WITH_SCOPE` | `VERIFIED_WITH_SCOPE` (Requires independent `VERIFIER` receipt or CI/replication) |
| `[REMOTE-CI]`, `[PHYSICAL-MEASURED]`, `[EXTERNAL-REPLICATED]` | `VERIFIED_WITH_SCOPE` | Requires `ROLE != PROPOSER` *(Blocks Proposal $\to$ Authority)* |

### B. Constrained Decoding (GBNF Grammar)
For local inference via `llama.cpp` / `vLLM`, ENLG v2 is clamped directly onto the token sampling logit mask via GGML BNF grammar:

```bnf
root ::= claim ws scope ws role ws evidence ws anti_promotion ws nulls ws falsifier ws validity ws verdict ws residual ws next_experiment

claim ::= "CLAIM:" ws text
scope ::= "SCOPE:" ws text
role ::= "ROLE:" ws ("PROPOSER" | "VERIFIER" | "AUTHORITY" | "EXECUTOR" | "OBSERVER")
evidence ::= "EVIDENCE:" ws ("- " [^\n]+ "\n")+
anti_promotion ::= "ANTI-PROMOTION:" ws ("- " [^\n]+ "\n")+
nulls ::= "NULLS:" ws "- M0_NO_EFFECT:" ws text "\n" "- M1_NOISE:" ws text "\n" "- M2_MEASUREMENT_OR_CONFOUNDER:" ws text
falsifier ::= "FALSIFIER:" ws text
validity ::= "VALIDITY:" ws text
verdict ::= "VERDICT:" ws ("UNKNOWN" | "CONFLICT" | "FALSIFIED" | "SUPPORTED_WITH_SCOPE" | "MEASURED_WITH_SCOPE" | "VERIFIED_WITH_SCOPE")
residual ::= "RESIDUAL:" ws text
next_experiment ::= "NEXT_EXPERIMENT:" ws text
ws ::= [ \t\n\r]*
text ::= [^\n]+
```

### C. ENLG Compact Wire Mode (Zero-Copy Frame Mapping)
Under heavy UMA saturation ($0.78$ threshold), generative tokens consume VBlank slots. ENLG compacts into the 128B `KrystalZeroCopyFrame`:
* `role_id` (4 bits) $\to$ `flags[0..3]`
* `evidence_bitmask` (8 bits) $\to$ `cap_scope[0..7]`
* `verdict_id` (4 bits) $\to$ `flags[4..7]`
* `receipt_digest` (32 bytes) $\to$ `hmac_token`
