## 1. Executive Strategy: Broad Audience Resonance Without Epistemic Compromise

Most deep-tech projects fail to reach a broad audience because their documentation is either:
1. Too opaque and academic (alienating developers, investors, and decision-makers), or
2. Too fluffy and marketing-heavy (losing technical credibility with seasoned engineers).

**Krystal Stack's Copywriting Framework** solves this by establishing a **Dual-Layer Communication Architecture**:
- **Layer 1 (The Hook & Value Bridge):** High-impact headlines, psychological empathy with pain points, and undeniable ROI metrics.
- **Layer 2 (The Epistemic Foundation):** Mathematical proofs, CEL (Commercial Evidence Levels), and reproducible zero-regression test evidence.
