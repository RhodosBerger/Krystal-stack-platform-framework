### Preamble: The Two False Gods of Modern Software

Modern computing stands paralyzed between two intellectual extremes:

1. **The Dogma of Static Determinisim:**
   The traditional engineering establishment clings to static lookup tables, fragile if/else heuristics, and rigid statecharts. In their fear of unpredictability, they cripple software’s ability to adapt. When the real world throws non-linear, multi-dimensional anomalies at their static systems, their code stumbles, throttles, and deadlocks.
2. **The Cult of Unconstrained Stochasticism:**
   The new AI avant-garde preaches that billions of neural parameters should directly drive the world. They give probabilistic black-box models raw API keys, root access, and physical actuator controls, celebrating "autonomous agency" while quietly ignoring the catastrophic reality of hallucinations, non-reproducibility, and systemic drift.

**We reject both false gods.**

Static rules are too blind to govern complex realities. Probabilistic models are too unstable to hold physical authority.

We declare a new foundation: **Epistemic Computing**.

### The Seven Pillars of Epistemic Governance

#### I. Separation of Thought and Act (Kognícia ≠ Autorita)
Every reasoning engine—whether a 70B parameter language model, a deep neural network, or a mathematical solver—has the fundamental right to observe, diagnose, synthesize, and propose.
**No reasoning engine has the right to act.**
Hypotheses are non-binding suggestions. Only the immutable, deterministic authority gate retains the sovereign right to commit changes to the physical state.

#### II. Budgeted Epistemics (Ekonómia inteligencie)
Intelligence is not free. Burning thousands of GPU watt-hours and millions of tokens to solve an incident that a 64-bit mask can resolve in 15 nanoseconds is an engineering crime.
Reasoning must be planned, metered, and budgeted. A system must think only as deeply as the entropy of the problem demands.

#### III. Attested Falsification (Zákaz halucinačných slučiek)
A system that learns from its own unverified assumptions descends into madness.
In Krystal Stack, no node may update its weights or adjust its beliefs based on synthetic outputs. Downstream learning operates strictly over the quantified difference between predicted expectations and empirical ground truth, sealed by cryptographic actuation receipts.

#### IV. Polymorphic Equality of Mind (Rovnosť výpočtových foriem)
We recognize no vendor monopoly. In our runtime, a 10-line C bitmask function, a high-order differential solver, a local ONNX model, and a cloud-hosted causal LLM stand as equal citizens. All speak the same immutable dialect: `IEpistemicNode`.

#### V. The Inviolable Zero Veto ($A_{\text{commit}} = 0$)
When authority reduction yields zero, the answer is absolute: **NO**.
There is no heuristic wiggle room. There is no soft fallback that pretends to have been approved. A fail-safe transition is a constitutional event, enacted in the light of day.

#### VI. Dynamic Workspace as Living Projection (Generatívny povrch)
We reject the primitive paradigm of conversational chat text for system governance. Software is not a chat partner; it is an instrument.
The user interface must be a dynamic, typed projection of the system’s internal epistemic state—rendering causal trees, decision matrices, live simulations, and evidence panels on the fly.

#### VII. The Constitutional Invariant ($V_{\text{max}} \equiv 6$)
In an age of unbounded abstraction drift, every system requires an immovable anchor. Our invariant—`VITAL_MAX_HP == 6`—is enforced across every state vector, envelope, receipt, and proof. It is our permanent reminder that complexity must always bow to invariant integrity.
